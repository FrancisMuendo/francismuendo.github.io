Overview

What I worked on

Built a beneficiary estimation workflow to support programme planning by combining available population, eligibility and programme assumptions into structured estimates.

Contribution

Key work

  • Structured input assumptions and population data.
  • Created transparent estimation logic that can be updated as assumptions change.
  • Produced outputs suitable for reporting and planning.
  • Designed the model with practical decision-making and scenario testing in mind.
Outcome: The model provides a repeatable approach for estimating potential programme reach and supporting resource-planning conversations.
