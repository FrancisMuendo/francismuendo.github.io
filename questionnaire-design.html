Overview

What I worked on

Designed structured questionnaires for quantitative and mixed-method research, balancing research objectives with respondent experience and field requirements.

Contribution

Key work

  • Translated research objectives into measurable questions and indicators.
  • Built screening, skip patterns and logical routing.
  • Structured questionnaires for different respondent segments.
  • Reviewed instruments for clarity, consistency and data quality.
  • Aligned questionnaires with downstream analysis and reporting requirements.
Outcome: Strong questionnaire design creates cleaner data at source and reduces avoidable errors during fieldwork, processing and analysis.
