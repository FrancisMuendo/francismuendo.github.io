Case study

Muzala Consultancy Company Website

Web DevelopmentUI/UXResearchAgriculture
Overview

What I built

Designed and built a professional website for Muzala Consultancy, an upcoming research and agricultural consulting company in Angola. The project focused on presenting the company clearly online, communicating its service areas and creating a credible digital presence for prospective clients and partners.

Contribution

Key work

  • Planned the website structure around the consultancy’s services and positioning.
  • Designed a clean, professional interface for an emerging consulting brand.
  • Developed responsive pages suitable for desktop and mobile users.
  • Organised research and agriculture-focused content into a clear user journey.
  • Created a foundation that can be extended with additional company information and services.
Outcome: The website gives Muzala Consultancy a professional digital front door and a platform through which potential clients and partners can understand the company, its focus and its services.
Why it matters

Using technology to strengthen how research and consulting organisations communicate their value, credibility and services online.
