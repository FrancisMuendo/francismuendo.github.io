Case study

Power BI for Business Intelligence using Nairobi Stock Exchange Data

Power BIDAXBusiness IntelligenceNSE
Overview

What I worked on

Designed an executive dashboard to help users quickly understand Nairobi Securities Exchange market performance. The solution brings market-cap, revenue, profit and PE-ratio indicators together with sector rankings and year-over-year return trends.

Contribution

Key work

  • Structured NSE data for executive reporting.
  • Built sector performance rankings and quarterly performance views.
  • Created KPI cards for market capitalization, revenue, profit and average PE ratio.
  • Added narrative insights to highlight outperforming and underperforming sectors.
  • Designed an executive-facing layout with filters for sector and company.
Outcome: The dashboard converts a broad market dataset into a decision-oriented executive view, making it easier to identify leading sectors, weaker segments and changes in market performance over time.
Why it matters

From analysis to action
