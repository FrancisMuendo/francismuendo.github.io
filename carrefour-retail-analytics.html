Case study

Carrefour Kenya Retail Analytics Dashboard

Power BIDAXExcelRetail Analytics
Overview

What I worked on

Built an executive retail analytics dashboard to monitor commercial performance and identify store, regional and promotional opportunities.

Contribution

Key work

  • Prepared and modelled retail data using Excel and Power BI.
  • Created DAX measures for sales, gross profit, margins and promotional performance.
  • Analysed sales by year, quarter, store and region.
  • Built customer and promotion performance views.
  • Added recommendations focused on top-performing stores, underperforming regions and margin protection.
Outcome: The dashboard gives decision-makers a single view of retail performance and highlights where commercial teams can focus investment, promotions and customer-retention activity.
Why it matters

From analysis to action

The dashboard gives decision-makers a single view of retail performance and highlights where commercial teams can focus investment, promotions and customer-retention activity. The broader approach reflects my focus on making analytical work understandable, defensible and useful to decision-makers.
